Milestone 12 elevates AniCare AI from an individual-animal clinical decision-support tool into an **enterprise geospatial disease intelligence and risk-mapping platform** for State Animal Husbandry Departments (DAHD), district epidemiologists, and cooperative dairy federations.

AniCare AI **never** claims that an outbreak is confirmed solely from AI computer-vision predictions or map clusters. AI outputs represent suspected syndromic telemetry that guides priority field inspection by certified veterinarians.

## 3. Geographical Hierarchy & Privacy-Safe Location Model

### 3.1 Normalized Location Structure
Each livestock event is associated with administrative boundary metadata:
```json
{
  "state": "Gujarat",
  "district": "Anand",
  "block": "Anand Rural",
  "village": "Boriavi",
  "latitude": 22.6105,
  "longitude": 72.9902,
  "location_accuracy": "CENTROID",
  "location_source": "ADMINISTRATIVE_CENTROID"
}
```

### 3.2 Location Privacy Guarantees
- **No Fabricated GPS**: The platform never invents real-world coordinates. Where coordinates are unmapped, `latitude = null, longitude = null, location_accuracy = ADMINISTRATIVE`.
- **Household Anonymization**: Individual farmer names, door numbers, or private sheds are **never exposed** on maps or public endpoints.
- **Centroid Aggregation**: Map points plot administrative centroids (Gram Panchayat headquarters or Taluka centroids) representing entire settlements.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
  - **Farmers (HTTP 403 Forbidden)**: Blocked from government-wide disease maps and cross-village hotspot registries.
  - **Veterinarians**: Authorized to access operational village/block risk overlays and hotspot dossiers.
  - **Administrators**: Full access to district/state-wide intelligence, recalculation engines, and demo seeding.

## 4. Transparent Spatial Risk Scoring Engine

The risk score is bounded strictly between $0$ and $100$ and resolved into 4 discrete tiers:
- **0–29**: `LOW`
- **30–59**: `MODERATE`
- **60–79**: `HIGH`
- **80–100**: `CRITICAL`

### 4.1 Configurable Factor Weights
All factor weights are defined in a single centralized dictionary (`RISK_FACTOR_WEIGHTS`):
| Factor | Weight | Maximum Points | Epidemiological Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Case Burden** | 25% | 25.0 pts | Scale of active caseload within the temporal window. |
| **Recent Growth** | 20% | 20.0 pts | Velocity of case accumulation vs. previous period. |
| **Geographic Density**| 20% | 20.0 pts | Concentration of infected animals within settlement radius. |
| **Clinical Severity** | 15% | 15.0 pts | Proportion of pyrexic, critical, or recumbent animals. |
| **Signal Strength** | 10% | 10.0 pts | Average AI vision confidence or veterinary validation. |
| **Affected Herds** | 10% | 10.0 pts | Lateral spread across multiple distinct pastoral sheds. |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100.0 pts** | Transparent linear additive formulation. |

### 4.2 Zero-Division Safe Growth Metric
$$\text{Growth \%} = \begin{cases} 
\text{"NEW\_SIGNAL"} & \text{if } \text{previous\_cases} = 0 \text{ and } \text{current\_cases} > 0 \\
0.0\% & \text{if } \text{previous\_cases} = 0 \text{ and } \text{current\_cases} = 0 \\
\frac{\text{current\_cases} - \text{previous\_cases}}{\text{previous\_cases}} \times 100\% & \text{if } \text{previous\_cases} > 0
\end{cases}$$
Zero division never results in `NaN`, `null`, or infinity.

## 5. Hotspot Intelligence & Priority Classification

### 5.1 Hotspot Intelligence Statuses
- **`EMERGING`**: New signal detected with no previous cases; early monitoring advised.
- **`ACTIVE`**: Sustained caseload within observation window.
- **`ESCALATING`**: Sharp increase ($\ge 50\%$ growth with $\ge 3$ active cases).
- **`DECLINING`**: Decreasing caseload following veterinary intervention.
- **`RESOLVED`**: Caseload normalized to zero within observation period.

### 5.2 Hotspot Priority Matrix
- **`P1 CRITICAL`**: Risk Score $\ge 80$, or Score $\ge 70$ with `ESCALATING` status. Priority for rapid response within 24 hours.
- **`P2 HIGH`**: Risk Score $60–79$. Priority inspection within 48 hours.
- **`P3 MODERATE`**: Risk Score $30–59$. Regular twice-daily syndromic observation.
- **`P4 MONITOR`**: Risk Score $< 30$. Baseline diary hygiene and sentinel monitoring.

## 6. Spatial Spread Analysis

The spread analysis engine compares active hotspots across adjacent settlements within the same block or revenue district.
- **Conditions Evaluated**: Proximity ($\le 5$ km), temporal lag (cases emerging 2–5 days after primary index cluster), and contiguous pastoral corridors.
- **Status Classification**: `SPREAD_SIGNAL_DETECTED` vs. `NO_CLEAR_SPREAD_SIGNAL`.
- **Mandatory Non-Definitive Phrasing**:
  - Valid: *“Geographical spread pattern detected between adjacent pastoral corridors; field verification recommended.”*
  - Prohibited: *“Disease has spread from Village A to Village B.”*

## 11. Known Limitations & Scientific Boundaries

1. **Screening Scope**: The current production vision model (`anicare_vision_bovine_v1.onnx`) is validated for bovine Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD) screening. Other syndromic conditions (e.g. Mastitis, FMD) are categorized via veterinary clinical telemetry, not visual AI inference.
2. **Decision Support, Not Confirmation**: Geospatial clustering indicates elevated statistical risk; regulatory epizootic disease confirmation requires formal RT-PCR or ELISA laboratory diagnostics.
3. **No Fabricated GPS**: Satellite coordinate overlays map administrative centroids only. Private farm locations are never forged.
4. **Spatial Proximity vs. Transmission**: Spatial clusters do not prove biological transmission routes; contact tracing and vector entomological surveys are required for epidemiological proof.
5. **Permission Controls**: External clinical image datasets (e.g. Dataset B) remain in legal quarantine until formal Data Transfer Agreements (DTA) are executed.
