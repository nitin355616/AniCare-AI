## The Pitch (Word Count: ~105 words, Duration: ~30 seconds)

> "In India, livestock epidemics like Lumpy Skin Disease take two weeks to be reported through traditional paperwork, devastating rural incomes and milk supply.
>
> **AniCare AI** transforms this into a Day-Zero early warning and rapid response system.
>
> Farmers report symptoms instantly in Hindi or English, even with zero internet connectivity.
> Our computer vision and clinical AI calculate an immediate risk index, while geospatial density algorithms detect emerging village hotspots before they explode.
> We coordinate the entire government response: mobile vet dispatch, cold-chain lab sample tracking, and authorized ring vaccination.
>
> **Detect. Assess. Map. Alert. Investigate. Respond. Monitor.**
>
> AniCare AI: saving livestock, protecting farmers, and securing India’s rural economy."
